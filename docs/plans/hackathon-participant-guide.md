# Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon — Participant Guide

Source: https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/details (captured 2026-10-26). Remote, 10 days. All times ET.

## Problem statements (must build toward one)

- **Build for Your World**: bring Meta's models into your domain. Solve a problem you know firsthand, free up time and mental space, and make possible something that wasn't before.
- **Push the Frontier**: use Meta's models on challenges in software, robotics, manufacturing, energy, materials and beyond.

**Must use Meta models.** Through the Meta Model API: the Muse Spark series, Muse Wonder, Muse Image, Muse Voice Transcribe and SAM 3.1. Open weights: Muse Glimmer and Muse Spark Open. Coding agent: Muse Code.

## Rules

- Public repo (open source) required.
- Max team size 4; solo is allowed.
- The demo must show only what was built during the hackathon, and new work must be clearly identified. If it isn't, judges can disqualify the project.
- Startups may build on existing code but must mark clearly what is new.
- Banned: projects that violate legal, ethical or platform policy, or that use code, data or assets you have no rights to.
- Anti-projects to avoid: medical advice AI, basic RAG, simple image analyzers, simple "AI for Education" chatbots, job application screeners, nutrition coaches, personality analyzers, sports analyzers or coaches, anything political or involving war.

## Submission

- Due **Nov 4, 9:00 PM ET**. One submission per team, entered by a named Representative. Late submissions are not considered.
- Requires a public repo, an accessible **3-minute demo video** (shows only what was built during the hackathon), and all team members listed.
- Judges use the video, code and live product.

## Judging

1. **Pass/fail**: fits the theme and makes reasonable use of the Meta API or open-weight models.
2. **Rubric scored 0–10**:
   - **Impact, 30%**: real-world potential, who benefits, fit with a problem statement
   - **Use of Meta AI tooling, 25%**: creative use beyond a basic integration
   - **Technical depth and execution, 20%**: sound, refined engineering
   - **Demo, 25%**: working, impressive, holds up live, good video
3. **Final round**: top 5 per track, ranked live on the same rubric.
